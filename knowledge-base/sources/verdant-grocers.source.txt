## Scenario

Verdant Grocers is a regional chain. Store managers currently reorder from a
report that is refreshed overnight, so a product that sells out at 10 AM is not
visible as out-of-stock to the replenishment team until the next morning.

What is known:

- 140 stores across three states.
- Each store has point-of-sale terminals that already emit a transaction event
  per basket. The events reach an on-premises message broker today.
- Roughly 900,000 basket events per day across the chain, with a pronounced
  weekday-evening peak.
- The merchandising team wants store-level stock positions visible "within a few
  minutes" of a sale, and has said that a figure closer to one minute would
  change how they staff replenishment.
- There is an existing overnight batch warehouse that finance depends on. It
  cannot be switched off, and finance's monthly close must not regress.
- The company has two full-time data engineers. There is no platform team.
- Leadership has said it does not want to be "locked into one vendor", but has
  not defined what that means in practice.
