### SOURCE: issue #14 — [Scenario] Northbridge Logistics - fleet reefer telemetry & alerting

## Business Context
Northbridge Logistics operates a fleet of 3,200 refrigerated ("reefer") trucks. Onboard IoT devices already report engine temperature, GPS location, reefer-unit temperature, and engine fault codes. Today this data only feeds an on-premises fault-code system used by the warranty-claims team, and nobody monitors reefer temperature in near-real-time.

## Business Objectives
1. Reduce unplanned roadside breakdowns that cause refrigerated-load spoilage.
2. Give fleet managers near-real-time visibility into reefer-unit health across the fleet.
3. Establish a data foundation that a future predictive-maintenance ML initiative (out of scope for this phase) can build on.

## What the System Must Do (Functional Requirements)
- Ingest telemetry (engine temperature, GPS, reefer-unit temperature, fault codes) from each truck's onboard device, sent roughly every 30 seconds per truck.
- Detect when reefer-unit temperature breaches a configured safe-range threshold.
- Alert the responsible fleet manager within 5 minutes of a threshold breach.
- Store historical telemetry for at least 2 years to support warranty disputes with the truck/reefer-unit manufacturers.
- Provide a dashboard showing currently active alerts per truck/route.

## Operating Conditions / Known Numbers
- 3,200 trucks fleet-wide, each sending telemetry approximately every 30 seconds.
- Alert must reach the fleet manager within 5 minutes of a threshold breach.
- Telemetry retention: minimum 2 years.
- Pilot phase: 400 trucks, targeted within 4 months. Full fleet rollout timeline after the pilot is not yet decided.
- No fleet-wide growth-rate projection has been provided yet (fleet size may grow but no number is available).

## Constraints
- Team: 6 backend engineers + 1 data engineer, comfortable with Python and SQL; two of them have prior production experience with Kafka.
- The existing on-premises fault-code system cannot be decommissioned yet — the warranty-claims team still depends on it and there is no committed migration date.
- No cloud vendor has been chosen yet; the company is open to any provider or a hybrid approach.
- Budget for this initiative has not been disclosed yet.

## Security / Compliance Notes
- Legal is still reviewing whether any regulatory regime applies to vehicle telemetry in the jurisdictions Northbridge operates in; no determination has been made yet.
- Fleet managers and warranty-claims staff need different views of the data, but exact role definitions haven't been finalized.

## Anything Else Known
- The reefer-unit temperature threshold values themselves are configured per cargo type by an existing rules engine that will keep being used; this project only needs to consume its output, not replace it.
