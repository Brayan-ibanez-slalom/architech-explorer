SPACE
Software Architecture & Systems Design
A regional airline is rebuilding its direct sales channel. The current one is ten years old, the vendor that built it no longer supports it, every change takes months, and
sales keep leaking to online travel agencies. The new platform is where a passenger searches for flights, books them, pays, adds seats and baggage, and later
comes back to change what they bought. It is not the airline's operating system: flights, seats, fares and cabin maps live in systems that will not be replaced.
The airline flies inside Colombia and to five countries in the region, with routes to the United States approved for month 18. Web and mobile app, same capabilities on
both, around 65 percent of traffic on mobile.

S C O P E
What the platform must do
01 Search and book
Find flights, create a booking, hold the seats until payment confirms or the
hold expires.
02 Seats and baggage
Pick seats from the cabin map and add baggage, during the booking or at
any moment after it.
03 Upgrade to business
Release the economy seat, take a business one, change the baggage
included, invalidate the seat already chosen, charge the difference.
04 Consult a reservation
Retrieve a booking with code and last name. This is the door to 02 and 03
after the sale.
05 Controlled overbooking
Sell above physical capacity inside a margin commercial sets per route
and season, changed without a deployment.
06 Payment with PSE
The passenger pays at their bank, and the confirmation comes back
asynchronously.
